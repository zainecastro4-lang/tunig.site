Frequently Asked Questions

What audio services do you offer?

Podcast Editing, Voice & Narration Editing, Mixing & Mastering, Music & Sound Design, Jingles & Audio Branding, and Custom Audio Projects.

Do you work with clients outside the Philippines?

Yes. We provide professional audio services for clients and projects worldwide.

What types of audio can you edit?

Podcast audio, voice and narration recordings, music, video-related audio, commercial audio, branded audio, and other custom audio projects.

Can you remove background noise?

Yes, background noise reduction can be part of the editing process when appropriate. Results depend on the original recording quality.

Can you improve the clarity of a voice recording?

Yes. Voice editing can include processing intended to improve clarity, consistency, balance, and overall listening quality.

Is mixing and mastering only EQ and compression?

No. Mixing and mastering can involve EQ, dynamic processing, saturation, stereo processing, limiting, automation, and other techniques.

Can you add background music and sound effects?

Yes. Background music, sound effects, transitions, atmospheres, and other creative audio elements can be included.
