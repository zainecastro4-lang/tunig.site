About Tunog Modern PH-Worldwide

Professional Audio Services Since 2018

Tunog Modern PH-Worldwide is a professional audio services business founded by Fredie Macalegal Ecaldre.

Fredie's audio experience dates back to 2018, with work focused on audio editing, processing, music-related production, and other audio projects.

The goal of Tunog Modern PH-Worldwide is to provide professional audio services for clients and projects worldwide.

What We Do

  • Podcast Editing
  • Voice & Narration Editing
  • Mixing & Mastering
  • Music & Sound Design
  • Jingles & Audio Branding
  • Custom Audio Projects

Each project is approached according to its specific requirements.

Our Approach

A recording may need cleanup, editing, processing, creative sound design, mixing, mastering, or a combination of techniques.

The approach is adapted according to the source material, project requirements, intended use, and desired result.
