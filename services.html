Mixing & Mastering

More Than Just EQ and Compression

Professional mixing and mastering involves more than simply applying EQ and compression.

Depending on the project, the process may involve:

EQ & Frequency Processing

  • EQ, dynamic EQ
  • Frequency balancing and resonance control
  • Multiband processing

Dynamics Processing

  • Compression, multiband compression
  • Expansion, gating
  • Transient processing

Saturation & Harmonic Processing

  • Saturation, harmonic enhancement
  • Excitation, distortion when appropriate

Stereo & Spatial Processing

  • Stereo imaging, panning, width processing
  • Reverb, delay, spatial processing

Loudness & Final Processing

  • Limiting, loudness optimization
  • Peak control and final format preparation
